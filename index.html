About Joe

Joseph James Bartolozzi, also know as Joe Bart, was born on February 6, 2002(age: 24). He is mainly known for his older TikToks(The Beenie Guy to be exact), where he would react or duet with other people, and would be yelling almost the entire time. On August 22nd, in 2020, Joe began his journey through streaming. From there, he gained massive amounts of viewers, followers, and subscribers. Though, others like Caseoh, Jynxzi, and Kai Cenat have way more followers, viewers, and subscribers, Joe Bart is still widely known when it comes to streamers, mainly because hes funny, and ripped.

Facts about Joe

  • He is instantly recognized by his trademark white Carhartt beanie, which he would wear in most of his past videos.
  • On July 2024, a TikTok of him reacting to a bear tearing the skin of a fish gained 14 million views.
  • He popularized the viral catchphrase "I've been in the hills(bleep) dudes," originating from a video where he mocked video game tropes.
  • Before fame, he was a competitive track and field athlete at St. Augustine Preparatory School, winning medals in high jump and hurdles.
  • He graduated from Stockton University magna cum laude with a degree in arts, philosophy, and religion.
  • He also unofortunetly, has Alpha-Gal Syndrome, a condition contracted via tick bites that causes allergic reactions to red meat.
  • He used his platform for charity, including raising $14,000 for the Palestine Children's Relief Fund in 2024 and donating $10,000 to three charities upon reaching 3 million subs.
  • In 2024, he won the Best Variety Streamer Award at The Streamer Awards and recieved a Best Just Chatting Streamer nomination in 2025.
  • As of 2026, he has gained over 25 million followers on TikTok, 5.3 million subscribers on YouTube, and over 2.5 million on Twitch.
  • Joe originally started on Musical.ly, before moving to TikTok, where he blew up.
